Presentation figures: DDD and RevBayes, 1 October 2026

Each figure is supplied as a high-resolution PNG and editable vector SVG.
01_rate_laws: linear, power-law and original exponential-in-N curves.
02_tree_and_hidden_diversity: extant tree versus full simulated diversity.
03_likelihood_agreement: 300 matched points across 100 simulated trees.
04_likelihood_profile: paired likelihood profiles for the illustrative tree.
05_estimation_variation: all 100 one-parameter fits; triangles mark bounds.
06_cutoff_convergence: a separate deliberate truncation stress case.

New simulations: 25 replicates per model-age combination; linear/power;
crown ages 3/8; lambda0=0.8, mu=0.2, K=15; complete extant sampling.
Fits estimate lambda0 only, fixing mu and K, within [0.20001,8].
These figures show a numerical benchmark and a small estimation pilot.
They do not demonstrate joint parameter recovery or fossil-MCMC validity.

Exact data, code and methods are in validation/DDD_presentation/ in the
RevBayes development checkout. The PDF report is output/pdf/ddd_simulation_report.pdf.
